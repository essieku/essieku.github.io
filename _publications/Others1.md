excerpt: '"Buy Now, Pay Later (BNPL) promises instant gratification, but does it quietly erode financial resilience across generations? Drawing on U.S. household data, this study uncovers that BNPL use is strongly tied to unhealthy short-term financial behaviors like mounting credit card debt and weaker emergency savings particularly among older cohorts. Yet, its influence on long-term planning, such as investment and retirement, appears negligible. By revealing how hyperbolic discounting shapes financial choices differently for Boomers, Gen X, Millennials, and Gen Z, this paper highlights an urgent need for tailored financial literacy programs and regulatory oversight to safeguard households in an era of frictionless credit" (Joint with Prince Bosompem), <em> Revise &amp; Resubmit <em>'
