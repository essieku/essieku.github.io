  When a finance app offers a market outlook, whom do users trust more, an AI system or a human advisor? Does frequent app use shift confidence toward AI, and does that differ across age, education, or financial experience? We aim to map where trust flows in the mobile finance era and what that means for product design, investor education, and consumer protection. (Joint with Prince Bosompim and Gary Mottola)
