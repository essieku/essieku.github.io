### Abstract
*Coming soon.* This study uses double machine learning to estimate the mortality consequences of concurrent obesity and diabetes across occupational settings, accounting for high dimensional confounding, interactions, and selection. Results highlight substantial excess risk from the obesity–diabetes double burden, especially in occupations with greater physical strain or hazardous exposure, and motivate integrated policy combining clinical management with workplace risk mitigation.
